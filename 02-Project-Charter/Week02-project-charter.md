---

Marcus Webb

## Business Need
The problem of RCHN is that we have an aging system that affects our intake system and scheduling, this is across 6 clinics. Solving this problem matters because it strengthens the foundation of the intake and scheduling system across all 6 clinics. In addition to synergizing with staff needs - improving training, workflow, and patients service goals.
---
## Purpose and High-Level Scope
the purpose is to replace the aging patients intake and scheduling system that is being used across 6 clinics. the risk is the reliability and efficiency of workflow will diminish.

the scope of the project is replacing the patient's intake and scheduling system. collecting the status of workflow at the 6 RCHN clinics

## Success Criteria
the first observable outcome for RCHN project is staff will be able to perform intake and scheduling on a new system. the second observable outcome is verifying that the clinics have transition off the old system. lastly is the checking the consistency of the work, making sure that the clinics are all working the same way or differently.
