# User Persona and Product Vision: RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System
**Consultant:** Marcos Lara
**Role:** Associate Consultant, Product Manager, NovaTech Consulting
**Engagement:** Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN)
**Date:** September 16, 2026
---
## User Persona
**Name:** Maria
**Role:**Medical Assistant 
**Years in Role:** 3 – 7 years

**Tech Comfort Level:** Medium, comfortable with common tools 
---
### Primary Work Goal
the goal is to check in patients and prepare patient information quickly and accurately to work quickly with the clinicians
---
### Main Frustration with the Current System
staff has to type the same patients information over and over because the system does not carry over the patients data across systems. What should be a single intake because of a multiple step process. On top of that, staff don't know if the information/appointment is saved.
---
### What Would Make Their Work Better
the system that intakes patients information carrying over every screen ( ) that needs it and with confirmation of saved information. Ensuring that there is no workaround for patient information and double checking if data was saved.


---
## Product Vision Statement

The first choice I made in my product vision statement is to keep the user type broad, using medical assistants and the front desk staff. across the RCHN location. This comes from the problems the medical assistants had to re-enter patients' data, confirm information or appointment, and having no shared network across the 5 RCHN. The outcome will have staff experience data to only be entered once which will save a lot of time, data shared, and mitigate errors.
