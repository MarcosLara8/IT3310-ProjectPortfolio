## Engagement Strategy Reflection
### Highest Priority Stakeholder
The stake holder in my register that needs the most careful attention is the RCHN IT team. I believe that the IT team can be easily overlooked because they do not have that much authority to make the final decision in the project.
If they were overlocked the project could possibly lead to doing a rework of the system/project or delay. The project could decide on picking a cloud system that isn't able to run across the 6 clinics which will push back the project deadline.

### Risk of Omission
One stakeholder that I don't think that I included is the RCHN patients. I did not think to include them because patients will be interacting with the front desk or intake coordinators who are already working at the front desk. Patients are not the ones who are going to be interactive with the intake system or scheduling appointments.
I think I would add patients if the project showed that it will include patients' interactive features. Such as translations or to self schedule your own appointment.
